# Barista She Wrote — Rebrand Blueprint

> **Café & Chapters.** A cozy-noir specialty café where every cup has a plot.

## 1. Research & positioning

**Finding:** "Barista She Wrote" has no indexed web presence (web search returned nothing), so this is a rebuild from the name, not an audit of an existing identity. Replace the assumptions below with real facts (location, current menu, prices) when you have them.

**The name is the asset.** It's a pun on *Murder, She Wrote* — instantly memorable, bookish, female-led. The old generic "coffee shop" lane is crowded; the whitespace is **literary cozy-mystery café**.

| Lane | Typical competitor | Barista She Wrote |
|---|---|---|
| Specialty minimalist | white walls, Scandi, cold | warm, moody, story-driven |
| Chain | speed, volume | slow, ritual, "stay for a chapter" |
| Book café | generic reading nook | noir/mystery theatre with a Gulf-flavoured menu |

**Audience:** 22–38, design- and culture-literate, Instagram-first, study/work-from-café crowd, gift-and-photo seekers.

**Brand idea:** *Every cup has a plot.*
**Pillars:** Story (menu as a mystery novel) · Craft (specialty beans, real ingredients) · Warmth (hospitality, Gulf flavours: saffron, cardamom, dates, pistachio) · Wit (puns, never kitsch).

**Voice:** dry, warm, one-liner wit. Detective-novel puns, used sparingly. Short sentences. *"Some cases stay cold. Your coffee shouldn't."*

## 2. Visual identity

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink Navy | `#14213D` | primary background, signage |
| Parchment | `#F4ECDC` | paper, light backgrounds, text on navy |
| Aged Brass | `#B8893B` | frames, rules, accents |
| Oxblood | `#6B1E2B` | secondary / "clue" colour, Red Herring |

**Type:** high-contrast serif for headlines (Playfair Display / Bodoni-style), italic for the "She Wrote" flourish; clean grotesk (e.g. Inter / Jost) for body; wide-tracked small caps for labels. Arabic companion: Amiri / Reem Kufi for lockups.

**Graphic motifs:** fountain-pen nib, ink swirl as steam, keyhole, red thread, case-file tab, brass frame lines.

**Logo options generated** (`assets/manifest.md` has URLs):
- **A – Badge:** pen-in-cup circular badge, "Café & Chapters" (recommended primary)
- **B – Wordmark:** oxblood italic "She Wrote" with ink-stroke bean underline (menus, packaging)
- **C – Monogram:** BSW keyhole (cups, stamp, favicon)

*Recommendation:* A as primary, C as stamp/favicon, B for menu headers. Rebuild the chosen one as a true vector before print — the generated files are raster concepts.
